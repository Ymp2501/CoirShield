# Simulated buyer panel (internal planning tool — not for the public site)

This is NOT customer research. It is a planning aid built from AI-generated personas.
Do not show it to judges as evidence, and never quote a persona as a customer.

- Panel: 20 AI-generated personas (not real people).
- 2 of 20 "bought" the original pitch.
- 9 of 20 "bought" once the offer included their own water test, an independent lab report and a re-test guarantee.
- Reading: trust looked like the main barrier. Treat this as a hypothesis to test in real conversations.
- Limitation: the panel tested a kirana-sale version of the offer, not SHG doorstep delivery.

Use it to decide what to ask real households and SHG members, then replace it with what they actually say.
